Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cancellation window

You may cancel an order free of charge within two minutes of placing it. After two minutes, please call the cafe immediately; cancellation may not be possible once preparation has started.

Cancellation by the cafe

We may cancel an order if an item is unavailable, the requested area cannot be served, or an operational issue prevents fulfilment. If you paid online for a cafe-cancelled order, we will arrange a full refund.

Refund eligibility

Online payments: eligible refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after approval.

COD orders: no refund is needed when a COD order is cancelled before delivery and no payment has been collected.

Wrong or missing items: contact us within 30 minutes of delivery. We may offer a replacement or refund after reviewing the issue.

Food quality issues: contact us immediately with the order number. Photos are appreciated and help us investigate quickly.

Non-refundable cases

We generally cannot refund a change of mind after food preparation has begun or a dine-in no-show. We will still review genuine service or safety concerns fairly.

How to request a refund

Call or WhatsApp 095619 79727 with your order number, registered email, phone number, and a short description of the issue.

Processing time

Approved online refunds usually appear within 5–7 business days, depending on the payment provider and your bank.

Contact
