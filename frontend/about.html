Our story

Made with madhuram.

“Madhuram” means sweet — and that's how we like to serve every plate.

A neighbourhood corner, done right.

Geeta's Madhuram Cafe brings the flavours of South India to Kaneri, Bhiwandi. From crispy Benne Dosa to Andhra-style Pesarattu and soft Uttapam, every plate is made fresh to order — served with sambar, chutney and a lot of love.

We opened on Agra Road, beside Tirupati Hospital, and quickly became the neighbourhood's corner for authentic South Indian delights. Today, our regulars include families, hospital staff, students, and anyone who wants a proper meal at a fair price.

Everything is cooked fresh every morning. The dosa is crisp, the Pesarattu is hearty, the chutneys are bright, and the portions are generous. That's the whole idea.
