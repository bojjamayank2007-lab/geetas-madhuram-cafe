Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What data we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order history, review content, and information you provide when contacting us.

Why we collect it

We use this information to create and secure your account, fulfil orders, contact you about an order, improve our service, respond to requests, and maintain appropriate business records.

Cookies

When you sign in, we use an HttpOnly customerToken cookie to keep you signed in. The authentication token cannot be read by page JavaScript. We do not store JWT tokens in localStorage.

Third parties

Razorpay may process online payments when that flow is enabled. MongoDB Atlas may store application data for the service. Swiggy handles orders placed through its own platform. These providers process information under their own policies and applicable agreements.

Data retention

We retain order history and related records for accounting, customer service, safety, and legal requirements. You may request deletion of your customer account data, subject to records we must retain by law.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact the cafe using the phone number below and include enough information for us to identify your request.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Authentication uses HttpOnly cookies, and production deployments should use HTTPS. We apply access controls so customers can view only their own orders.

Children's privacy

This service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations, or data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact
