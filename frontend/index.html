Our story

Good food, served the simple way.

Geeta's Madhuram Cafe brings the flavours of South India to Kaneri, Bhiwandi. From crispy Benne Dosas to Andhra-style Pesarattu and soft Uttapams, every plate is made fresh to order — served with sambar, chutney and a lot of love.

We opened on Agra Road, beside Tirupati Hospital, and quickly became the neighbourhood's corner for authentic South Indian delights.

Whether it's a quick morning dosa, an evening plate of Mirchi Bajji, or a full Poori Kurma meal — we cook it the way it's meant to be cooked. No shortcuts.
